Practice areas

A focused practice, not a scattered one.

Four areas, worked properly, rather than a long list worked thinly. Each one below covers what Mikeya actually does and what a typical matter looks like.

01 — Contracts & agreements

Agreements you can rely on later

Most disputes trace back to a clause nobody read closely at the time. Mikeya drafts and reviews agreements in plain language first, then in proper legal form — so both sides know what they've agreed to.

  • Residential and commercial lease agreements
  • Employment contracts and staff policies
  • Service and supplier agreements
  • Partnership and shareholder agreements
  • Non-disclosure and non-compete terms

02 — Property & conveyancing

Due diligence before you commit

Land disputes are expensive to unwind after the fact. Mikeya checks title, reviews sale agreements, and manages the transfer process so you know exactly what you're buying into — or out of.

  • Title search and due diligence
  • Sale and purchase agreements
  • Transfer and registration documents
  • Lease reviews for tenants and landlords
  • Boundary and title dispute guidance

03 — Family & succession

Handled with discretion

Wills, powers of attorney, and succession matters are personal by nature. Mikeya works through them at a pace that respects the family, with the paperwork done properly the first time.

  • Will drafting and review
  • Powers of attorney
  • Succession and estate administration guidance
  • Family agreements and separation matters

04 — Small business counsel

Legal footing for a growing business

Registration, basic compliance, and ongoing advice — the legal groundwork most small businesses need but few budget properly for. Mikeya keeps it practical and scaled to the size of the business.

  • Business registration and structuring
  • Compliance basics and regulatory filings
  • Ongoing retained legal advice
  • Contract templates for repeat use

How a matter proceeds

What to expect, in order

Initial consultation

A plain-language conversation about what's at stake, either in person or remotely. Fees are discussed here, upfront.

Review and advice

Mikeya reviews the documents or facts of the matter and gives clear advice on the options and risks involved.

Drafting or action

Agreements are drafted, filings prepared, or the agreed next step is carried out, with drafts shared for your input.

Signing, swearing, or filing

Where a document needs to be sworn or certified, that happens in person, with ID checked and the oath properly administered.
