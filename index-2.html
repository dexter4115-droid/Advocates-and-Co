Commissioner for Oaths

Sworn documents, done properly

As a Commissioner for Oaths, Mikeya witnesses signatures and administers oaths and affirmations on affidavits, statutory declarations, and sworn statements — so your paperwork holds up wherever it's needed.

Bring valid photo identification and the unsigned document. Sign only once instructed, in Mikeya's presence.

  • Affidavits

    Sworn written statements for court, immigration, or administrative use.

  • Statutory declarations

    Formal declarations where no oath is required but truthfulness must be certified.

  • Certified true copies

    Verification of copies against original documents for official submission.

Common questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an appointment to get a document sworn?

Yes — book ahead so Mikeya can set aside proper time. Walk-ins are accommodated when possible, but appointments are prioritised.

What should I bring to have an affidavit commissioned?

Valid photo identification (national ID or passport) and the unsigned document. The document must be signed in Mikeya's presence, not before.

How much does a first consultation cost?

Fees depend on the matter and are always confirmed upfront before any work begins — ask when you book and you'll get a clear figure, not an estimate that moves.

Can Mikeya represent me outside Nairobi?

Yes. The office is based in Nairobi, but matters and remote consultations are handled countrywide where the case requires it.
