About

A younger voice in the profession, held to the same old standards.

Mikeya Muluka is 31 years old, an advocate of the High Court, and a Commissioner for Oaths. Since being admitted to the bar, Mikeya has built a practice around the matters that affect people directly — their homes, their agreements, their families — approaching each one with the same care whether the file is large or small.

What sets a younger advocate apart isn't a shortcut on rigor; it's patience for explaining things plainly, and comfort with the paperwork-heavy reality of modern legal work — contracts, filings, and the affidavits and declarations that need a Commissioner for Oaths to stand behind them.

Standing & credentials

What Mikeya is licensed to do

Advocate of the High Court of Kenya

Admitted to practice law, providing legal advice and representation on contracts, property, family, and small business matters.

Commissioner for Oaths

Authorised to administer oaths and affirmations, and to witness affidavits, statutory declarations, and sworn statements.

Member, Law Society of Kenya

Bound by the Society's professional conduct rules and continuing legal education requirements.

Approach

How Mikeya works

Every matter starts with a plain-language conversation about what's actually at stake, before any document gets drafted or any advice gets given. Fees and timelines are discussed upfront, not buried in the fine print.

When a document needs to be sworn or certified, that step happens carefully and in person — identification checked, the document read and understood before it's signed, and the oath administered properly.
